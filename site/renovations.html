Frequently Asked

Renovation Questions, Answered

Can we live in the home during a renovation?

For partial renovations, often yes — with clear site protocols around dust containment, work hours, and access. For whole-home remodels, we usually recommend temporary housing and coordinate timing accordingly.

How long does a renovation take?

A focused kitchen runs 10 to 16 weeks. A primary suite, 8 to 14 weeks. Whole-home renovations typically run 6 to 14 months depending on scope and permitting.

Do you discover hidden issues mid-project?

Older homes hold surprises — that's normal. Our pre-construction review is designed to surface them early. When something genuinely unexpected appears, we document it, price it, and decide together how to proceed.

Will my permit history be clean?

Yes. Every BARC renovation is properly permitted and inspected. That protects your investment and ensures a clean record at resale or refinancing.

Do you handle small jobs?

We focus on substantial renovations where the scope justifies the discipline of our process. For very small jobs we're happy to refer you to a trusted contractor in our network.
