## Daily story system

Pick 2–3 per day, never all 5:

- **Morning:** a single project frame (close-up of a finish, a plan markup, a site photo) with one line of context.
- **Midday:** poll or question sticker to lift reach.
- **Afternoon:** repost of a tagged homeowner, architect, or interior designer.
- **Evening:** quote card from the hook bank or a client line with permission.
- **Anytime:** countdown sticker to an upcoming reveal.

---

## Growth ops (run alongside content)

- **Mon 9 AM:** reply to every DM and comment from last week within 24 hr. Response speed in luxury = perceived service quality.
- **Wed:** personally DM 5 engaged followers ("Appreciate the note on our Palo Alto build — what brought you to our page?"). Don't pitch. Build a human thread.
- **Fri:** 2–3 collab posts per month — with architects, interior designers, landscape architects, or realtors in your service cities. Reciprocal tags outrank ads in this niche.
- **Sunday audit:** pull reach, saves, profile visits, link taps per post. Double down on the top 20%, kill the bottom 20%.
