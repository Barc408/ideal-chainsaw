## Daily story system

Pick 2–3 per day, never all 5:

- **Morning:** jobsite photo or short clip with caption ("Day 43 — roof dry-in today").
- **Midday:** poll or question sticker to lift reach.
- **Afternoon:** repost user-tagged content or sub spotlight.
- **Evening:** quote card or hook from hook bank.
- **Anytime:** countdown sticker to upcoming reveal or carousel.

---

## Growth ops (run alongside content)

- **Mon 9 AM:** reply to every comment from last week within 24 hr.
- **Wed:** DM 5 tagged accounts from your posts ("Appreciate the tag — where are y'all building?").
- **Fri:** 3 collab posts per month — other builders, suppliers, or local realtors.
- **Sunday audit:** pull reach, saves, shares for each post. Double down on the top 20%, cut the bottom 20%.
