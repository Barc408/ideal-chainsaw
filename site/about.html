Our Story

Built on Hard Work. Defined by What We Build.

BARC Builder Group was founded in the mid-2010s on a simple belief: that the Bay Area deserved a construction company that combined genuine craftsmanship with the kind of work ethic that can't be taught — only lived. Over 13 years and 75+ completed residences later, that belief is the foundation of everything we build.

Founder & Principal

Preston Falbo

Preston didn't come to construction — he grew up inside it. From the time he was old enough to hold a tool, he was on job sites, absorbing the craft from the ground up and developing an eye for detail that can only come from years of hands-on experience.

He built BARC from nothing — no inherited business, no outside capital, no shortcuts. What he had was an intimate knowledge of how great homes are built, a relentless work ethic shaped by a childhood where nothing was given, and a deep connection to the Bay Area communities he grew up in.

I grew up watching what it takes to build something that lasts. That's never left me — and it shows up in every home we build. — Preston Falbo, Founder
Core Values

What We Stand On

Craftsmanship Above All

We hold every joint, finish, and detail to a standard that goes beyond code compliance. Because a home built with genuine care is simply better — and our clients can feel it.

Invested in This Community

Our Team

Every Person on a BARC Job Site Earns Their Place There.

Behind every BARC project is a team of craftspeople, project managers, and trusted subcontractors who share the same standards that founded this company. We don't staff up with whoever is available — we work with the best people we know, many of whom have been part of our projects for years.
