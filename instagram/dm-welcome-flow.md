# BARC Builder Group — DM Welcome Flow

Audience: Bay Area luxury homeowners.
Tone: warm, professional, never salesy. In luxury, a bad DM is worse than no DM.
Tool: ManyChat or IG's native quick replies. Start with native — simpler, no third-party dependency.

---

## Flow 1 — "START" keyword (primary lead funnel)

**Trigger:** user comments `START` on any "comment START for the first-90-days guide" post.
**Goal:** email capture + warm them into a consult conversation.

### Message 1 (sent immediately)
> Appreciate you reaching out. Sending over our *First 90 Days: What to Decide Before You Hire Anyone* guide now.
>
> What's the best email for the PDF?

### Message 2 — after they reply with email
> Just sent it — check spam if it isn't there in a minute.
>
> If it's useful, I'd love to hear which city you're looking at. (No obligation. We read every reply.)

Human reply from here — not automated. This is where the relationship starts.

---

## Flow rules

- **No third-person "we" in the first message** — "I" feels human, "we" feels corporate. Shift to "we" in message 2+.
- **First-name personalization only.**
- **Never send 3 automated messages in a row** without human intervention. The whole point is to open a conversation — not to run a bot.
- **Reply to organic DMs within 1 hour during business hours.** In luxury, response time is a proxy for build quality in the prospect's mind.
- **Every auto-reply ends with an open question** to invite a response, not close one.
- **Kill flows that underperform.** If fewer than 35% of triggered users reply with an email, rewrite the message.

## Qualification signals to watch for (forward to founder)

When any of these appear in a DM, treat it as a hot lead and route to the founder within the hour:

- Mentions a specific city in your service area.
- Mentions an architect by name (Bohlin Cywinski, Arcanum, Feldman, etc.).
- Mentions a lot address or says "our lot."
- Asks about availability or "when could you start."
- Mentions a specific timeline under 24 months.
- Says "we're comparing builders" or names a competitor.
