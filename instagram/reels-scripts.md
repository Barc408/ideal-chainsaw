# BARC Builder Group — Instagram Reels Scripts

Goal: grow followers / awareness
Niche: home & construction builders
Format notes: 15–30s vertical, text-on-screen for sound-off viewers, save-worthy hook in first 1.5s.

---

## 1. "The $40 Fix That Saves $4,000"

**Hook (0–2s):** "This $40 part prevents the #1 call-back on new builds."
**Beats:**
- B-roll: close-up of flashing being installed around a window.
- VO: "Most leaks don't come from the roof. They come from window heads."
- Show correct flashing overlap + shingle detail.
- "Skip it and you're replacing drywall in year two."
**CTA:** "Follow BARC Builders for the details GCs don't post."
**Caption:** Framers: tag the PM who still skips head flashing 👀 #framing #homebuilder #constructionlife
**On-screen text:** "$40 now or $4,000 later."

---

## 2. "Jobsite Walk — Spot the Mistake"

**Hook (0–2s):** "There are 3 code violations in this photo. Can you find them?"
**Beats:**
- Slow pan over a framed wall / rough-in.
- Timer counts 5 seconds.
- Circle each issue with a quick zoom: missing hurricane tie, improper notch, wrong stud spacing.
- Payoff: "How many did you catch?"
**CTA:** "Drop your count in the comments. Follow for weekly spot-the-mistake."
**Caption:** If you got 3/3 you belong in the BARC builder group. Comment your score.
**On-screen text:** "3 violations. 5 seconds. Go."

---

## 3. "Tool I Won't Build Without"

**Hook (0–2s):** "This tool paid for itself on day one."
**Beats:**
- Reveal the tool (e.g. laser level, track saw, impact driver, speed square).
- 2 quick clips of it saving time on the jobsite.
- "Used to take 20 minutes. Now it's 90 seconds."
**CTA:** "Save this. Share with your apprentice."
**Caption:** What's the one tool that changed your jobsite? Comment 👇
**On-screen text:** "Tools that actually earn their spot in the truck."

---

## 4. "Day in the Life — Framing Crew"

**Hook (0–2s):** "5 AM to lock-up. What a framing day actually looks like."
**Beats:**
- Quick-cut montage: coffee → blueprint check → wall layout → raising walls → lunch truck → sheathing → end-of-day walkthrough.
- Hard cuts on the beat, nat sound.
**CTA:** "Follow for more real jobsite days, no filters."
**Caption:** Real hours, real crew, real build. This is why we do it.
**On-screen text:** Timestamps in the corner for each clip.

---

## 5. "Rookie vs. Veteran"

**Hook (0–2s):** "Rookie hangs a door in 40 minutes. Veteran does it in 8. Here's why."
**Beats:**
- Split-screen or A/B cut.
- Rookie: shims everywhere, checking level 6 times.
- Vet: 3 shims, one check, done.
- Tip callouts: "Set the hinge side first." "Shim behind each hinge." "Gap the top before the sides."
**CTA:** "Tag a rookie who needs this."
**Caption:** The fastest way to look like a pro? Hang doors like one.
**On-screen text:** "40 min → 8 min"

---

## 6. "Before / After — 30 Seconds"

**Hook (0–2s):** "Same house. 90 days apart."
**Beats:**
- Hold on the "before" shot for 2s.
- Time-lapse-style cuts: demo → framing → rough-in → drywall → finish.
- Final reveal with a slow push-in on the finished space.
**CTA:** "Follow BARC Builders for the full build story."
**Caption:** 90 days. One crew. Swipe through the process on our page.
**On-screen text:** "Day 1 → Day 90"

---

## 7. "Myth-Busting Monday"

**Hook (0–2s):** "No, you don't need permits to DIY this. Wrong."
**Beats:**
- Text overlay of the myth.
- Quick to-camera from a builder: "Here's what actually happens when you skip the permit…"
- Show real consequence: failed inspection, insurance denial, resale issue.
**CTA:** "Comment the myth you want us to bust next."
**Caption:** Bad advice on IG costs real money. Follow for the truth.
**On-screen text:** "MYTH: Permits are optional."

---

## Posting cadence (suggested)

- Mon: Myth-busting
- Wed: Jobsite walk / spot the mistake
- Fri: Day-in-the-life or tool pick
- Sun: Before/After reveal

- "Stop doing [X] on [Y]. Do this instead."
- "[N] things I'd tell my apprentice self."
- "Why pros [do X] — and you should too."
- "If your crew still [does X], show them this."
