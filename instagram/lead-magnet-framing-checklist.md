## Page 2 — Structural

Walk the frame with this list in hand. Check each item before calling the inspection.

### Walls
- [ ] Studs plumb within 1/8" over 8 ft.
- [ ] Top plates doubled, splices lapped minimum 4 ft.
- [ ] Bottom plates on pressure-treated where required (slab, exterior).
- [ ] Anchor bolts at 6 ft on center max, 12" from each end of plate.
- [ ] Anchor bolt washers: 3" x 3" x 0.229" min (most jurisdictions).
- [ ] Shear wall nailing matches the engineered schedule — not guessed.
- [ ] King studs and trimmers correct per header span.
- [ ] Notching: max 25% of stud depth on bearing, 40% non-bearing.
- [ ] Boring: holes max 40% of stud depth, min 5/8" from edge.

### Headers & openings
- [ ] Header size matches plans for span + load.
- [ ] Proper jack (trimmer) count under each end of header.
- [ ] Cripples installed above header where required.
- [ ] Window/door RO dimensions match the ordered units (measure before you close up).

### Floor & roof framing
- [ ] Joist hangers correct model, fully nailed (all holes).
- [ ] Joist bearing min 1.5" on wood, 3" on masonry.
- [ ] Blocking at mid-span where joist depth requires it.
- [ ] Rim joist continuous, properly nailed to plate.
- [ ] Truss bracing per truss engineer's sheet — not improvised.
- [ ] Hurricane ties / uplift connectors at every truss bearing.
- [ ] Rafter ties or ceiling joists in place to prevent wall spread.
- [ ] Ridge beam sized for load, properly supported at bearing.

## Page 3 — Life safety, envelope, MEP coordination

### Fire blocking
- [ ] Fire blocking at all concealed vertical/horizontal intersections.
- [ ] Soffits and dropped ceilings blocked.
- [ ] Penetrations through plates sealed (caulk, foam, or mineral wool per code).
- [ ] Around chimney chases, stair stringers, and tub/shower platforms.

### Draft stopping
- [ ] At 1,000 sqft intervals in floor framing where required.
- [ ] Continuous at attic-to-wall transitions.

### Envelope
- [ ] Sheathing nailed per schedule (edge + field spacing right).
- [ ] Sheathing gaps at panel edges (1/8" typical — check spec).
- [ ] House wrap lapped shingle-style, taped at seams.
- [ ] Window/door flashing: sill pan first, jambs over sill, head flashing last.
- [ ] Roof underlayment overlapped correctly, ice-and-water shield where required.

### MEP coordination (before drywall)
- [ ] No plumbing or HVAC in exterior walls where insulation depth matters.
- [ ] Drilled holes and notches don't compromise structural members.
- [ ] Bath fan vented to exterior, not into attic.
- [ ] Dryer vent path clear, no flex inside wall cavities.
- [ ] Backing installed for towel bars, grab bars, TVs, cabinets, handrails.

## Page 4 — Final walk + bonus

### 15 minutes before the inspector arrives
- [ ] Plans on site, updated with any RFI answers.
- [ ] Engineering stamped sheets on site.
- [ ] Job site clean — clear paths to every corner.
- [ ] Ladders set where upper framing needs to be accessed.
- [ ] Previous inspection corrections signed off.
- [ ] One person assigned to walk with the inspector — not three.
- [ ] Phone off. Respect the process.

### The "usually fails" shortlist
These are the top 5 reasons jobs get red-tagged in most jurisdictions:
1. Missing fire blocking at soffits / chases.
2. Wrong nailing pattern on shear walls.
3. Over-notched studs for plumbing.
4. Missing hurricane ties on trusses.
5. Improper header sizing on garage doors.

If you're clean on those five, you're ahead of 80% of the field.
