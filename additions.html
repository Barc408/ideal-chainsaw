Frequently Asked

Addition Questions, Answered

How do I know if I can build an ADU on my lot?

California ADU law gives most homeowners the right to build at least one ADU, but specifics depend on your city and lot. We confirm zoning eligibility, setbacks, and lot coverage as part of the feasibility step before design begins.

How long do additions take?

Typical detached ADUs run 8 to 12 months from groundbreaking. Attached additions vary more with scope but generally fall in the 6 to 10 month range.

Will the addition look like a remodel job, or part of the original home?

Part of the original home. Matching proportions, rooflines, materials, and detail is fundamental to how we design and build additions. The seam should disappear.

Can I rent out the ADU?

In most jurisdictions, yes — long-term rental is permitted. Short-term rental rules vary by city. We'll point you to the relevant local regulations.

What about utilities and permits?

We manage permitting, inspections, and any required utility upgrades on your behalf. Every addition we build is fully permitted with a clean record.
