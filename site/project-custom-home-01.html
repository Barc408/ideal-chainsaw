Custom Home · 2025

Custom Home No. 01

A modern California residence in Los Gatos — designed for a family who wanted the home to disappear into its hillside, then reveal itself slowly through carefully composed views and quiet, durable materials.

Type
Custom Home
Location
Los Gatos, CA
Scope
Ground-Up · 6,200 sf
Year Completed
2025

The Challenge

The site sloped sharply across both axes and held mature oaks the family wanted to preserve. The brief called for an open, light-filled home that would also feel intimate, with an indoor-outdoor flow that worked through the long Bay Area dry season — and remained warm in the cooler months.

Our Approach

We worked closely with the architect to seat the home into the slope rather than fight it, breaking the program across three gentle terraces that follow the natural grade. We took a measured approach to materials: hand-troweled plaster walls, white oak flooring laid in long, single-piece runs, and steel windows specified with thermal-broken frames so the glass could be as generous as the family wanted.

The Result

A home that reads quietly from the street and opens dramatically to the rear landscape. The mature oaks were preserved. The material palette is restrained but considered — every transition resolved, every detail pulled toward the simplest solution that does the job. The clients tell us the home feels like a retreat, even when full of people.

Key Features

What Sets It Apart

Hand-Troweled Plaster

Lime plaster walls throughout the main living spaces — depth, warmth, and a quiet sheen no paint can replicate.

Continuous White Oak

Single-run rift-and-quartered oak floors, with grain and tone matched across rooms for visual continuity.

Steel Window Walls

Thermally broken steel doors and windows specified for the long western elevation, balancing solar gain and view.

Bespoke Millwork

Floor-to-ceiling cabinetry, paneling, and built-ins shop-built by the same team — tolerances kept in furniture territory.

Concealed Systems

Lighting, HVAC, and audio fully concealed in plaster reveals and millwork — no visible registers, no surface boxes.

Landscape Integration

Mature oaks preserved through phased site work; hardscape and softscape coordinated with construction throughout.
