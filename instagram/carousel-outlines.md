## Design system (keep it consistent)

- **Colors:** BARC orange (#F26A21) + black + off-white. High contrast only.
- **Font:** One bold display (e.g. Montserrat Black) + one clean body (e.g. Inter).
- **Cover slide:** 6–9 word headline, big numeral if listicle.
- **Last slide:** Always a CTA + @handle.
- **Alt text:** Write descriptive alt text on every slide for reach.
