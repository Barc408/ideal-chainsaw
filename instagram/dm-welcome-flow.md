# BARC Builder Group — DM Welcome Flow

Trigger: new follower, comment keyword (JOIN, CHECKLIST, SCHEDULE), or auto-reply on story reactions.
Tool: ManyChat, MobileMonkey, or IG's native quick replies (simpler, no third party).

---

## Flow 1 — "JOIN" keyword (primary group-recruit funnel)

**Trigger:** user comments `JOIN` on any post.
**Goal:** get them to the group signup link.

### Message 1 (sent immediately)
> Hey [first name] — appreciate you commenting 🙏
> The BARC builder group is open to GCs, framers, trim carpenters, PMs, and anyone who actually swings a hammer.
>
> Want the link?

**Buttons:** `Send the link` / `Tell me more first`

### Message 2a — if "Send the link"
> Here you go 👇
> [group signup URL]
>
> Two things once you're in:
> 1. Drop your city in the intro channel.
> 2. Post a jobsite photo — we'll shout you out.
>
> See you inside.

### Message 2b — if "Tell me more first"
> Fair. Quick rundown:
>
> 🔨 Weekly jobsite breakdowns + plan-reading walkthroughs
> 🧰 Tool reviews from people who actually use them
> 📈 Business of building — bids, subs, software, pricing
> 💬 Real builders, zero gurus
>
> Free to join. No pitch.
>
> Link?

**Buttons:** `Yes, send it` / `Not yet`

### Message 3 — if "Not yet"
> All good. I'll send one reel a week that might change your mind 👊
> (Follow if you haven't — that's where the good stuff lives.)

**Trigger:** user comments `SCHEDULE` on the 90-day carousel.

### Message 1
> Here's the trade schedule we used on that 2,400 sqft build 👇
> [link to PDF or Notion doc]
>

### Message 2 — human reply within 24 hr
Real response, not automated. This is where community lives.

## Flow 4 — New-follower welcome

**Trigger:** new follow.
**Goal:** prime the relationship without being spammy (IG throttles this, keep it short).

### Message 1
> Thanks for the follow 🙏
> I'm [founder name] — 20 years building custom homes in [city].
>
> If you're a builder, tap here to join the BARC group → [link]
> If you're looking to build a home, tap here → [consult link]

That's it. One message. No follow-ups unless they reply.

---

## Flow 5 — Story reaction auto-reply

**Trigger:** emoji reaction to a story.

### Message 1
> Appreciate the [emoji] 👊
> What caught your eye — the build or the crew?

Keeps the conversation human. Let the founder answer from here.

---

## Flow rules

- **One CTA per message.** Two options max. More than that kills reply rates.
- **First name personalization** only — IG last-name pulls look creepy.
- **Never send 3 automated messages in a row** without a human touch.
- **Reply within 1 hour during business hours.** IG rewards fast responders with reach.
- **Kill flows that underperform.** If fewer than 25% of triggered users click through, rewrite it.
