# DIY First Reel — 30 Minutes, Phone Only

Your first reel. No videographer, no editor. Just your phone, a jobsite, and a free app.

**Goal:** Post reel #2 ("Walk this Palo Alto build with us") by the end of today.

---

## What you need

- Your iPhone (or any recent smartphone).
- 15 min on an active or recently completed jobsite.
- Free app: **CapCut** (download from App Store — search "CapCut" by ByteDance).
- A cup of coffee. That's it.

---

## Part 1 — Shoot (10–15 min on site)

### Phone settings (do this once, first time only)

1. Open **Settings → Camera → Record Video** → set to **1080p HD at 60 fps**.
2. Open **Settings → Camera → Formats** → select **Most Compatible** (not "High Efficiency"). Saves you headaches in CapCut.
3. Turn on **Grid**: Settings → Camera → Grid → ON. Helps you frame shots level.

### How to hold the phone
- **Hold vertical (portrait mode).** Reels are vertical. Never shoot horizontal for IG.
- **Two hands.** Elbows tucked into ribs. This alone makes footage look 10x more professional.
- **Move SLOW.** Walk like you're carrying a full coffee. Slow = cinematic. Fast = amateur.

### The 6 shots to film (one each, 6–8 seconds per clip)

1. **Establishing wide** — stand at the property entrance or driveway. Frame the whole house or framing in one shot. Hold still for 6 seconds.

2. **Slow walk-in** — start 20 feet from the front door. Walk forward slowly, phone in front of you, centered. Film the approach.

3. **Looking up** — inside, point the phone up at the framing, ceiling, or roof structure. Slow pan or hold still.

4. **Detail shot** — get close (arm's length) to ONE thing: a steel beam connection, a window frame, a stair stringer, a tile sample. Hold still for 5 seconds.

5. **Walk-through** — slow steady walk from one room to the next, going through a doorway. One take.

6. **Ending wide** — step back outside or to a key interior space. Frame a wide "today's progress" shot. Hold 6 seconds.

**Total footage: about 40 seconds raw. You'll cut to 20–25 in editing.**

### Shooting rules (the only ones that matter)

- **Natural light only** on your first reel. Don't fight with lighting.
- **No one talking** — nat sound or silence. You'll add music in CapCut.
- **If a shot feels shaky, re-film it.** 2 seconds on the clock.
- **Film more than you need.** 2–3 takes of each shot. Pick the best one later.

## Part 2 — Edit in CapCut (15 min)

### Open CapCut, start a new project

1. Open CapCut → tap **New Project**.
2. Select your 6 clips → tap **Add**.
3. You're now on the editing screen.

### Trim each clip to 3–4 seconds

1. Tap a clip in the timeline at the bottom.
2. Drag the white edges inward to trim.
3. Keep the best 3 seconds of each clip.
4. Repeat for all 6. Total reel should be 18–24 seconds.

### Add your text overlays

Reel #2's on-screen text (from `reels-scripts.md`):

- Clip 1: "Week 9 of 52"
- Clip 2: "Palo Alto"
- Clip 3: "Steel moment frame — earthquake performance"
- Clip 4: "12-ft ceilings on main level"
- Clip 5: "Primary bath with canyon view preserved"
- Clip 6: "Follow the build. Fridays."

**How to add text in CapCut:**
1. Tap the clip.
2. Tap **Text** at the bottom → **Add Text**.
3. Type the line. Choose **Font: Inter Bold** or **Poppins Bold**.
4. Make text **white** with a subtle **shadow** (tap Style → Shadow).
5. Drag text to the lower third of the screen. Keep it simple.
6. Drag the text edges in the timeline to match the clip duration.

### Add music

1. Tap **Audio** → **Sounds**.
2. Search for these free options (type into search):
   - "cinematic ambient"
   - "slow piano"
   - "emotional instrumental"
3. Preview. Pick one that feels quiet and confident (not hype / trap / EDM).
4. Tap the checkmark to add.
5. Drag the audio timeline so it starts at clip 1 and ends at clip 6.
6. **Lower the volume to about 40%.** Tap the audio track → Volume → 40.

### End card (last 2 seconds)

1. Tap **+** at the end of your timeline.
2. Choose **Solid Color** → black.
3. Trim to 2 seconds.
4. Add text: **"@barcbuildergroup"** (gold color, centered).
5. Under it, smaller: **"Start your legacy build — link in bio."**

### Export

1. Top-right: tap **Export**.
2. Settings: **1080p, 30 fps, High quality**.
3. Saves to your phone's camera roll.

## Part 3 — Post to Instagram (5 min)

1. Open Instagram → tap **+** → **Reel**.
2. Tap **Upload from gallery** → select your new video.
3. Skip most options. Don't add IG music (you already added it in CapCut).
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Paste this caption:

```
Week 9 of 52. Palo Alto.

Steel moment frame going in today — earthquake performance you only see in the bill, and feel in the next quake.

Follow the build. New update every Friday.

#paloaltohomes #customhomebuilder #bayareaconstruction #barcbuildergroup #luxuryhomes #customhome #bayareahomes
```

6. **Cover photo:** tap Cover → scrub to your best frame (usually clip 1 or 5). Pick one that looks great as a still.
7. **Tag people:** tag your architect, interior designer, or any collaborator (huge for reach).
8. **Location:** tag "Palo Alto, California" (or whichever city).
9. **Advanced settings → Share to Facebook:** ON.
10. Tap **Share**. Done.

## First-time mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Filming horizontal. **Always vertical.**
- ❌ Using a shaky clip because you only filmed it once.
- ❌ Adding 6 different fonts. **One font. White. Shadow. That's it.**
- ❌ Loud music with a strong beat. Luxury = quiet score.
- ❌ Cramming 30 seconds of text. **2–5 words per overlay, max.**
- ❌ Clips longer than 4 seconds each. Viewers scroll on boring.
- ❌ No end card. Always end with your handle + CTA.

## If something breaks

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Clips are shaky | Refilm with elbows tucked to ribs. |
| Text is cut off on IG | Move text to the center-middle, not the edges. |
| Music is too loud | Lower volume to 30–40% in CapCut. |
| Clips look washed out | In CapCut, tap clip → Adjust → Contrast +10, Saturation +5. Don't overdo. |
| Export takes forever | Export at 1080p 30fps, not 4K. |
| Cover looks bad | Scrub through more frames. Pick one with no motion blur. |
