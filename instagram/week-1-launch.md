## Step 5 — Set up ONE DM auto-reply (10 min)

Start simple with IG's native Quick Replies (no third-party tools needed).

IG app → Settings → Business tools → **Saved replies** → Create new.

**Shortcut:** `bidchecklist`
**Message:**
```
Appreciate you reaching out. Our 12-question bid-comparison checklist is coming over — what's the best email for the PDF?
```

When someone comments "BID" on the Wednesday carousel, you'll get a notification. Open their DM and type `/bidchecklist` — the saved reply populates. Hit send, then continue the conversation human-to-human.

(Full automation via ManyChat can wait until Week 3. Native replies work fine for first 30 days.)
