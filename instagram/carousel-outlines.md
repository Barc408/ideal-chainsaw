## Carousel 3 — "5 Questions to Ask Before You Hire a Custom Home Builder"

- **Slide 1 (cover):** "5 questions that save Bay Area homeowners millions."
- **Slide 2:** Q1 — "Have you built in my city in the last 24 months?" Why it matters: each AHJ is different.
- **Slide 3:** Q2 — "How do you handle change orders?" Look for: fixed markup, written authorization, 48-hr pricing.
- **Slide 4:** Q3 — "What's your trade contractor retention rate?" Great GCs keep the same subs for years.
- **Slide 5:** Q4 — "Can I see a line-item budget from a finished project?" If they won't share (with names redacted), that tells you everything.
- **Slide 6:** Q5 — "Who is my single point of contact every week?" Ask for a name, not a role.
- **Slide 7:** Bonus — "Will you introduce me to 3 clients whose builds finished over a year ago?" Recent references are coached. Old ones are honest.
- **Slide 8 (CTA):** "Save this. Share it with the person building with you. Start your conversation with BARC → link in bio."

**Caption:** The best time to ask these questions is before the contract. The second-best time is right now.

## Design system (keep it consistent)

- **Colors:** BARC gold (#C9A24B) + black (#0A0A0A) + ivory (#F4EFE6). High contrast only.
- **Fonts:** One elegant serif (e.g. Playfair Display) for display + one refined sans (e.g. Inter) for body.
- **Cover slide:** 6–9 word headline, project number or city in small caps.
- **Last slide:** Always a CTA + @barcbuildergroup.
- **Alt text:** Write descriptive alt text on every slide for reach + accessibility.
- **No emojis** in the luxury carousel set. Save those for comments and stories.
