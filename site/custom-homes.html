Custom Homes

A Home Built Entirely Around You

A truly custom home is one of life's great privileges. We build ground-up residences of extraordinary quality — where every material, finish, and proportion has been considered with care, and every decision is made in service of the way you want to live.

Scope of Work

What a BARC Custom Build Includes

Site Strategy

Lot evaluation, grading analysis, view studies, and site planning to position your home for the best possible result.

Architectural Coordination

We work hand-in-hand with your architect — or recommend one from our trusted network — to keep design and constructability aligned.

Structural & MEP

Foundation, framing, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing coordinated by experienced specialists who deliver on the design intent.

Custom Millwork

Built-ins, paneling, and bespoke cabinetry crafted to the same standard you'd expect of a fine furniture maker.

Premium Finishes

Stone, hardwood, plaster, metalwork — sourced and installed by craftspeople who specialize in the material at hand.

Landscape Integration

The home and grounds designed and built as one. Hardscape, softscape, lighting, and water features fully coordinated.

Smart Home Systems

Lighting, climate, audio-visual, security, and shading — wired, programmed, and tested before you take possession.

Permitting & Compliance

Full management of permitting, inspections, HOA approvals, and code compliance from foundation through final.

Documentation Package

As-built drawings, warranty registrations, maintenance schedule, and complete permit records delivered at handoff.

Our Process

Six Steps. No Surprises.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A no-obligation conversation about your vision, lot, timeline, and budget. We listen first.

  2. 02

  6. 06

    Completion & Handoff

    Quality review, walkthrough, punch list resolution, and full documentation package.

Frequently Asked

Custom Home Questions, Answered

How long does a custom home take to build?

Most ground-up custom homes take 14 to 22 months from groundbreaking to final inspection, depending on size and complexity. We'll give you a realistic schedule before you sign.

Do you work with our architect, or do you have one to recommend?

Both. We collaborate with the architect of your choice and have long-standing relationships with several Bay Area firms whose work we admire if you're still selecting one.

How is the budget structured?

Every BARC custom home is built under a fixed-fee or cost-plus contract with a detailed line-item estimate. Allowances are clearly stated, and any change orders are priced and approved in writing before work proceeds.

What does your warranty cover?

We provide a comprehensive workmanship warranty alongside the manufacturer warranties for installed systems and materials. Specifics are documented in your handoff package.

Will I have direct access to the team during construction?

Yes. You'll have a single point of contact in your project superintendent, weekly progress updates, and an open invitation to walk the site whenever it's safe to do so.
